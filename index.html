The Project

In nature, everything moves with clarity and rhythm. Nothing is forced, yet everything unfolds as it should. We are not separate from this — we function in the same way, but have forgotten how to move with that flow. What we search for — clarity, direction, balance — is already present, reflected in the way nature grows and exists.

Awarded a Catalyst Grant, Biophilia is an ongoing project exploring human relationships with the natural world through painting, sound, and spatial design. It invites a shift in perception: from separation to connection. This work marks the beginning of a larger project that will continue to evolve.

Three Paintings

People

Belinda Person

Artist · Project Lead

Belinda Person is a painter whose work explores the continuity between the human body and the natural world. Through oil painting, she renders landscapes where figure and forest share one surface — inviting viewers to recognize themselves as part of, not apart from, the living environment.

Krystian Ramlogan

Professor · Research Collaborator

Krystian Ramlogan is a faculty researcher at the Georgia Institute of Technology working across immersive media, sound, and spatial design. His collaboration on Biophilia extends the paintings into sensory environments — asking how a painting can become a space rather than a static object on a wall.
